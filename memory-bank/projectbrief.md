# Project Brief

## Business / project description

TrackFlow is a logistics and fulfillment operations platform focused on end-to-end parcel movement across warehouses, distribution centers, transit hubs, and final delivery destinations. The business goal is to provide operational visibility over the full shipping journey so teams can understand how packages move between facilities, carriers, and delivery points in real time.

The project is centered on a real-world logistics environment where shipments are constantly routed between storage sites, regional hubs, and customer addresses. This includes operational complexity across warehouse handling, transfer between facilities, transportation mode changes, and final-mile delivery coordination. The platform is designed to support the people and systems responsible for keeping this flow running smoothly.

## Project objectives

- Create a unified view of shipment movement from warehouse to delivery.
- Improve operational visibility for warehouse, transit, and last-mile teams.
- Reduce service disruptions and delays by surfacing exceptions earlier.
- Support customer support teams with faster and more informed shipment updates.
- Build a scalable foundation for operational analytics, automation, and AI-assisted workflows.
- Enable cross-region and multilingual operations, including the realities of U.S. and Spain-based logistics workflows.

## Problem the project solves

Logistics teams often struggle with fragmented operations: package information is spread across warehouse systems, route planning tools, customer service channels, and regional handoff points. When a parcel is delayed or misrouted, operators may not know which stage failed, whether the issue is operational or customer-facing, or which team should respond.

This creates inefficiency, delayed communication, and poor customer experience. TrackFlow solves this by centralizing the operational picture around shipments, movement events, facility transitions, and service exceptions so teams can spot issues earlier and act with more confidence.
