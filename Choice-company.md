## My AI Agent Idea

My agent idea is that you need to focus your energy on organizing what needs to be done like a project manager, meaning make sure everything is accounted for, what parts of the plan will need to be emphasized and put into a hierarchy from most to least important.  The agent will be an aide to making the components that you want to put together, however, you must piecemeal it together and tell the agent one step at a time or else it will be too unfocused and try to do things out of control.  All in all, the agent will help you see all the options of how it might work, but you ultimately have to figure out if the agent is doing what you want it to do, and to guide the agent to what your overall goals are.
